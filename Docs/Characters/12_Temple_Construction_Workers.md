# Character Visual Dossier: Temple Construction Workers & Stone Masons (Kalthatchar & Uzhavar)

![Temple Construction Workers and Stone Masons](image%20copy%207.png)

- **Character ID:** CH-12 · **Canonical Tier:** Tier B (Civil Labor Archetype)
- **Role:** Granite Stone Masons, Sledge Haulers, and Ramp Construction Laborers
- **Evidence Tag:** `[HISTORICALLY ATTESTED]` (Built on documented workforce records for Thanjavur and Gangaikondacholapuram Brihadisvara Vimana construction per Doc 03 C1, G2)

---

## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age Range:** 20–45 years old.
- **Height / Build:** Lean, extraordinarily muscular and sinewy bodies developed through years of lifting granite monoliths, hammering chisels, and hauling rope teams. Powerful shoulder traps, calloused hands, thick forearms.
- **Skin Tone:** Very dark, sun-baked black-brown, coated from head to toe in pale beige-grey granite stone dust and sweat.
- **Facial Features:** Resolute, weather-beaten faces; white stone dust clinging to eyelashes, eyebrows, and mustache; focused craft expressions.
- **Hair:** Thick hair tied up in simple cloth-wrapped rags or shaved head with a small worker's tuft.

---

## 2. Workwear & Protective Gear
- **Clothing:** Minimalist coarse undyed cotton loincloth (*Kowpeenam* / *Veshti*) tied tight with hemp string around the waist to prevent snagging on scaffolding ropes.
- **Head Protection:** Folded coarse cotton cloth pad (*Summadu*) worn on the crown of the head to balance heavy stone headloads and prevent scalp injury.
- **Hand / Foot Protection:** Rawhide palm wraps for rope haulers to prevent friction blisters from giant coir pulling cables; bare feet with thick calloused soles adapted to rough stone yards.

---

## 3. Working Tools & Equipment
- **Stone Mason (*Kalthatchar*):** Hardened iron pointed chisel (*Uli*) and heavy wooden or bronze mallet (*Kottu-Uli*).
- **Ramp Hauler (*Uzhavar*):** Thick braided coconut coir ropes (*Kayiru*) looped across the chest for sledge team towing; wooden levers (*Kambu*).
