---

## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age:** 23–25 years old.
- **Height / Build:** 175 cm (5'9"), lean, wiry, agile athletic build. Built for mobility, climbing scaffolding, stone-yard maneuvering, and grounded practical defense rather than brute force.
- **Skin Tone:** Sun-bronzed, warm olive-brown with visible dust and salt traces from active fieldwork.
- **Facial Features:** Sharp, observant dark eyes with intelligent focus; straight, practical nose, slight scar near the left eyebrow from a childhood slate fragment; expressive, thoughtful mouth. Youthful but hardened by investigation.
- **Hair & Beard:** Thick wavy black hair tied back in a utilitarian, low neck-knot (*Kudumi*) to stay clear of eyes while drafting and climbing. Short, stubble-to-neat jawline beard that reflects long days on the construction site.

---

## 2. Costume & Visual Evolution Across Acts
- **Construction Site Investigator (Canonical In-Game Look per Reference Image):**
  - Durable, breathable sand-colored unbleached cotton *Veshti* wrapped with double tuck for high mobility.
  - Rust/terracotta patterned cotton waist sash with fringed terminals.
  - Leather tool harness across the chest with loops for palm-leaf stylus, charcoal rubbing stones, and small brass calipers.
  - Forearms bound in protective unbleached cloth wraps to prevent stone splinters when climbing scaffolding and inspection platforms.
  - Bare feet with calloused soles for maximum traction on granite scaffolding beams.

---

## 3. Jewelry & Functional Accessories
- **Necklaces:** Simple single-strand dark tulsi or rudraksha beads on a black cord with a small bronze family amulet.
- **Wrists:** Tough braided leather cord on right wrist; brass thumb ring used for scoring palm leaves and testing stone alignment angles.
- **Ear:** Small, understated silver stud (*Kadukkan*) in the left earlobe.

---

## 4. Personal Tools & Weapons
- **Writing Stylus (*Ezhuthani*):** Sharp iron-tipped brass stylus tucked in the waist belt, used for reading and incising Grantha/Tamil script on palm leaves.
- **Rubbing Stone Kit:** Small pouch containing black charcoal lumps and soft handmade rag-cloth for taking epigraphic stone rubbings.
- **Sidearm Weapon:** Short Chola bronze-hilted single-edged iron short-sword (*Vaal*) in a worn wooden sheath bound with rawhide, slung at the left hip for personal protection.
