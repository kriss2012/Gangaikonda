# Character Visual Dossier: The Architect (Chief Sthapati / Master Builder)

- **Character ID:** CH-04 · **Canonical Tier:** Tier B (Fictional built on documented guild master role)
- **Role:** Lead Architect & Structural Engineer of the Brihadisvara Vimana and Gangaikonda capital works
- **Evidence Tag:** `[PLAUSIBLE RECONSTRUCTION]` (Built on documented architectural engineering conventions per Doc 03 C2, G2)

---

## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age:** 46–50 years old.
- **Height / Build:** 178 cm (5'10"), sturdy, broad-chested, thick muscular forearms and strong calloused hands from decades handling plumb lines, chisels, and drafting boards. Heavy, grounded stance.
- **Skin Tone:** Dark deeply tanned brown, dusted perpetually with pale golden sandstone powder and granite dust.
- **Facial Features:** Stern, focused brow; piercing dark eyes that constantly measure proportions and level lines; prominent nose; square, resolute jaw. Intense, no-nonsense countenance.
- **Hair & Beard:** Dense curly black hair peppered with iron-grey, tied into a tight topknot secured by an oiled leather thong. Short coarse salt-and-pepper beard hugging the jawline.

---

## 2. Costume & Practical Workwear
- **Lower Garment (Veshti):** Durable, thick coarse unbleached cotton *Veshti* wrapped short and tucked securely between the thighs (*Kaccha* style) for climbing ladders and stone courses.
- **Work Belt:** Heavy rawhide leather carpenter's/builder's belt (*Kattazhai*) equipped with brass rings and pouches containing:
  - Lead-weight plumb bob (*Thooku Gundu*) on twisted hemp cord.
  - Carved ivory measuring rod (*Kizhi Muzham*) marked in imperial Chola angulas.
  - Right-angle bronze square (*Muttam*).
  - Hardened iron scribe for marking granite surfaces.
- **Upper Body:** Bare muscular torso with a dust-coated sweat cloth tied across the brow or slung over the right shoulder. Fine granite dust visible in chest hair and arm folds.
- **Footwear:** Thick water-buffalo hide sandals with brass studs on soles for grip on steep inclined timber ramps.

---

## 3. Demeanor & Body Language
- Constantly in motion: pointing at masonry joints, calculating inclined loads, squinting against sunlight to check vertical vimana alignments, gesturing demonstratively to stone-cutters and scaffold riggers.
