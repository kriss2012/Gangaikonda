# Character Visual Dossier: The Merchant (Ainnurruvar Guild Leader)

- **Character ID:** CH-06 · **Canonical Tier:** Tier B (Fictional built on documented merchant guilds)
- **Role:** High Representative of the *Tisai-Aayirathu-Ainnurruvar* (The Five Hundred of the Thousand Directions Trans-Oceanic Merchant Guild)
- **Evidence Tag:** `[PLAUSIBLE RECONSTRUCTION]` (Built on documented Ainnurruvar and Manigramam trade guilds per Doc 03 E1)

---

## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age:** 45–48 years old.
- **Height / Build:** 173 cm (5'8"), prosperous, well-fed but vigorous frame; dignified, expansive posture with confident, persuasive hand gestures.
- **Skin Tone:** Medium warm golden-brown, shielded from harsh outdoor sun by parasols and palanquins.
- **Facial Features:** Shrewd, perceptive dark eyes that appraise weights and values in an instant; groomed mustache with waxed tips; polite, welcoming, yet impenetrable commercial smile.
- **Hair & Beard:** Glossy black hair with subtle silver temples, conditioned with fragrant sesame oil and combed into an elegant side chignon (*Kudumi*).

---

## 2. Costume & Material Wealth (Subtle Luxury, Not Fantasy)
- **Lower Garment (Veshti):** Translucent, ultra-fine white Kanchipuram silk *Veshti* woven with delicate green and gold selvedge threads, falling gracefully to the ankles.
- **Upper Garment (Uttariya):** Rich forest green and raw saffron silk shawl draped across the left arm and chest, dyed with expensive imported natural pigments.
- **Jewelry:**
  - Gold signet ring carved with the sacred Ainnurruvar elephant-and-banner guild emblem on the right index finger.
  - Double strand of luminous Basra pearls interspersed with raw emerald beads around the neck.
  - Finely worked gold earstuds (*Kadukkan*) set with Burmese rubies.

---

## 3. Commercial Accoutrements
- **Brass Scales (*Thurukol*):** Miniature folding precision balance beam in a velvet-lined carved teakwood box.
- **Document Cylinder:** Gilded bronze screw-top cylinder holding trade charters signed by the Chola imperial treasury and foreign ports (Srivijaya, Kadaram, Song Dynasty China).
