# Character Visual Dossier: The Messenger (Royal Courier / Othai-Thoothan)

- **Character ID:** CH-10 · **Canonical Tier:** Tier B (Fictional built on documented courier network)
- **Role:** Fast Royal Dispatch Rider & Frontier Scout
- **Evidence Tag:** `[PLAUSIBLE RECONSTRUCTION]` (Built on documented Chola imperial highway and post-station network per Doc 03 D1)

---

## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age:** 22–26 years old.
- **Height / Build:** 172 cm (5'8"), lean, remarkably fit marathon runner/cavalry rider build; low body fat, powerful calves and thighs from days in the saddle.
- **Skin Tone:** Dark deeply sun-burned skin, perpetually coated in fine red road dust.
- **Facial Features:** Alert, wide observant dark eyes trained to scan road horizons and ambushes; wind-chapped lips; resolute, focused face.
- **Hair & Beard:** Short-cropped or tightly knotted black hair bound in a practical ochre cloth band. Clean-shaven cheeks with road-dust stubble.

---

## 2. Costume & Travel Equipment
- **Lower Garment:** Knee-length tight-bound buff cotton riding breeches (*Kaccha* tuck) reinforced with stitched leather patches along inner thighs to prevent saddle chafe.
- **Chest Wrap:** Ochre/terracotta dust sash wrapped tightly around the torso, carrying a bronze badge of the Imperial Chola Tiger (*Raja-Mudhirai*) certifying right of way on all imperial trunk roads.
- **Water & Rations:** Dried gourd water flask (*Surai-Kudam*) and rawhide pouch of parched grain (*Aval*) on hip.
- **Footwear:** Soft goatskin riding boots reaching mid-calf with rawhide laces.

---

## 3. Dispatches & Weaponry
- **Dispatch Cylinder:** Waterproof lacquered bamboo scroll tube slung diagonally across the back on a leather baldric, stamped with imperial vermilion wax.
- **Weaponry:** Light Chola short-spear (*Eetti*) and an iron curved utility knife on the waist belt.
