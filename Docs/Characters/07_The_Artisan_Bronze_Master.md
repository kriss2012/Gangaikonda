# Character Visual Dossier: The Artisan (Master Bronze-Caster / Sirpi)

- **Character ID:** CH-07 · **Canonical Tier:** Tier B (Fictional built on documented lost-wax bronze casters)
- **Role:** Master Sculptor (*Sirpacharya*) of Imperial Chola Processional Bronzes (Nataraja, Somaskanda)
- **Evidence Tag:** `[PLAUSIBLE RECONSTRUCTION]` (Built on documented Chola lost-wax casting technology per Doc 03 G1)

---

## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age:** 35–38 years old.
- **Height / Build:** 170 cm (5'7"), lean, muscular torso with sinewy arms, exceptionally steady hands, and thick calloused palms. Marked visibly by his craft: faint pale burn scars along the right forearm and shoulder from molten copper splatter.
- **Skin Tone:** Rich dark sienna brown, constantly smudged with charcoal dust, beeswax residue, and greenish verdigris patina powder.
- **Facial Features:** Patient, contemplative eyes accustomed to looking deep into hot glowing clay molds; gentle, focused demeanor.
- **Hair & Beard:** Thick curly black hair tied up in a working knot wrapped in a soot-stained grey cotton bandana to catch sweat. Short, rugged stubble.

---

## 2. Costume & Workshop Attire
- **Lower Garment:** Tough, coarse dark unbleached cotton loincloth / short *Veshti* stained with soot and clay wash, cinched tight with a hemp rope.
- **Protection:** Heat-resistant split-leather apron bib tied around the waist when ladling molten crucible alloys (*Panchaloha*).
- **Bare Feet:** Bare feet coated with fine dry foundry clay and casting sand.

---

## 3. Specialized Tools
- **Wax Carving Tools:** Fine bone and brass spatulas (*Mezhugu Karuvi*) used to carve intricate ornaments into the beeswax core before casting.
- **Crucible Tongs:** Long heavy black forged-iron tongs (*Kuradu*) leaning against the mud furnace.
- **Chisels:** Polished bronze and steel chasing chisels and small horn mallets for final cold-working of cast bronze faces and finger curves.
