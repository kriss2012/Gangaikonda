## 1. Physical Anatomy & Silhouette
- **Age:** 38–42 years old.
- **Height / Build:** 180 cm (5'11"), muscular, sea-hardened frame with wide athletic shoulders and broad stance ("sea legs") built to ride shifting decks in choppy ocean swells.
- **Skin Tone:** Dark, deeply sun-baked and wind-burned skin with salt residue.
- **Facial Features:** Squinting crow's feet from looking into glare on the open sea; sharp, hawkish eyes; scarred left jawline from an iron spear tip during a coastal landing skirmish.
- **Hair & Beard:** Long wind-blown black hair tied tightly into a high warrior bun wrapped in oiled indigo sailcloth. Coarse, trimmed chin beard and strong mustache.

---

## 2. Maritime Military Costume
- **Lower Garment:** Short, tight-wrapped indigo-dyed cotton fighting *Veshti* with gold geometric borders ending just above the knees, bound tight with a wide rawhide leather girdle and red waist sash.
- **Chest Protection:** Hardened boiled-leather chest guard (*Kavacham*) stitched with bronze studs over the vital organs, leaving shoulders and arms completely free for rowing and boarding maneuvers.
- **Footwear:** Barefoot with calloused grip soles for wet wooden deck planks.

---

## 3. Weapons & Maritime Equipment
- **Primary Weapon:** Heavy curved Chola marine cutlass/sword (*Aruval-Vaal*) with a brass tiger-head guard and sharkskin grip.
- **Secondary:** Small circular hardened leather buckler shield strapped to the left forearm.
- **Instrument:** Carved white sea-conch horn (*Sanku*) on a cord around the neck, used to blow fleet maneuvers through fog and monsoon rain.
